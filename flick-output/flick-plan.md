# Flick plan — MakeMoneyWithAI short

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920), 30 fps, 885 frames (29.5s)
**Style:** Anime visual grammar — cel shading, screentone halftone, radial speed lines,
impact frames, hard-cut snap timing, overshoot easing, chromatic split on impacts.
**Brand assets supplied:** none. Palette and type are original to this run.
**Palette:** ink `#0d0b14`, paper `#f4efe4`, hot pink `#ff2e63`, cyan `#22d3ee`, gold `#ffc53d`
**Type:** Anton (display slams), Archivo Black (labels), DejaVu Sans Mono (terminal/rows)
**Audio:** bundled action-matched SFX only. No background music.

---

## Scene 1 — `promise-flash`
- **Transcript:** "Everyone tells you AI will make you rich." (0.0–4.0s / frames 0–120)
- **On screen:** Radial speed lines burst from centre over a gold sunburst that rotates
  slowly. A coin-bright halftone dot field drifts up. The word **RICH** slams in at 3× scale
  with overshoot, chromatic-split for 3 frames, then settles.
- **Text on screen:** "EVERYONE SAYS" (small, top) / "AI WILL MAKE YOU **RICH**"
- **Assets:** none
- **Interaction:** none — this is a title impact frame
- **Sound:** `Impact.mp3` on the slam (frame 18)
- **Audio-coupled:** speed lines expand on the impact transient
- **Transition out:** white impact-frame flash, 4 frames

## Scene 2 — `empty-toolbox`
- **Transcript:** "Nobody tells you which tools actually do it." (4.0–8.5s / frames 120–255)
- **On screen:** Ink-dark screentone field. A row of six empty card outlines slides past
  right-to-left, each one dashed and hollow. A scanline sweeps down looking for content and
  finds none; each card stamps a small "?" as the scan passes. A hot-pink diagonal slash
  strikes through the whole row on the last beat.
- **Text on screen:** "NOBODY TELLS YOU **WHICH ONES**"
- **Assets:** none
- **Interaction:** simulated scan pass over the card row
- **Sound:** `Suspense.mp3` under the scan, `transitions.mp3` on the slash
- **Audio-coupled:** the slash lands on the transient
- **Transition out:** hard diagonal wipe, pink leading edge

## Scene 3 — `star-ranked-scroll`
- **Transcript:** "Five hundred open-source AI projects. Ranked by stars." (8.5–14.0s / frames 255–420)
- **On screen:** A vertical list of ranked repo rows scrolls upward at a steady clip —
  rank number, repo name, star count. Star counts tick up as each row passes the focus band.
  A gold star bursts at the focus line on each row. Halftone gradient behind, cel-shaded
  paper card in front. Counter in the corner races 0 → 500.
- **Text on screen:** ranked rows (AutoGPT 178.8k, stable-diffusion 157.0k, ollama 153.4k,
  transformers 150.5k, n8n 143.7k, langchain 116.5k, dify 115.6k, ComfyUI 89.8k) / "**500+** PROJECTS"
- **Assets:** none — row data is drawn from this repo's README
- **Interaction:** sequential scroll with per-row focus
- **Sound:** `Pop.mp3` on each of the six row crossings (frames 10, 30, 51, 71, 91, 112), `Correct.mp3` when the counter locks at 500 (frame 132)
- **Audio-coupled:** star burst fires with each pop
- **Transition out:** counter locks, screen punches in 8%

## Scene 4 — `rapid-fire-cards`
- **Transcript:** "AutoGPT. Ollama. LangChain. n8n. ComfyUI." (14.0–19.5s / frames 420–585)
- **On screen:** Five name cards, one per beat, hard-cut. Each card snaps in from an
  alternating direction with its own speed-line field and accent colour, holds 20 frames,
  then is replaced by an impact-frame flash. No cross-fades — anime cut timing.
- **Text on screen:** "AutoGPT" / "Ollama" / "LangChain" / "n8n" / "ComfyUI", each with its
  star count as a small kicker
- **Assets:** none
- **Interaction:** sequential card replacement
- **Sound:** `Pop.mp3` on each card entry (5×)
- **Audio-coupled:** the flash frame sits on each transient
- **Transition out:** final flash holds 3 frames into scene 5

## Scene 5 — `monetize-annotation`
- **Transcript:** "Every single one, with how people actually monetize it." (19.5–24.5s / frames 585–735)
- **On screen:** One repo card sits centre on paper stock. Three annotation lines draw
  outward from its edge to three labels that type in one character at a time:
  **SaaS**, **API**, **CONSULTING**. A gold underline wipes beneath the word "monetize".
- **Text on screen:** "ollama · 153.4k" card / "SaaS" / "API" / "CONSULTING" / "how people
  actually **monetize** it"
- **Assets:** none
- **Interaction:** sequential annotation draw + typing
- **Sound:** `Typing.mp3` under the label typing
- **Audio-coupled:** each line completes on a keystroke
- **Transition out:** labels hold, background darkens to ink

## Scene 6 — `pick-one-ship-it`
- **Transcript:** "Star it. Pick one. Ship it." (24.5–29.5s / frames 735–885)
- **On screen:** Ink background, drifting petal particles, rotating sunburst. A hollow star
  outline fills gold and bursts. Then three words slam in sequentially on the beat —
  **STAR IT** / **PICK ONE** / **SHIP IT** — each with its own speed-line burst. Ends on the
  repo wordmark with a cel-shaded underline.
- **Text on screen:** "STAR IT" / "PICK ONE" / "SHIP IT" / "MakeMoneyWithAI"
- **Assets:** none
- **Interaction:** none
- **Sound:** `aha-moment.MP3` on the star fill, `energy.MP3` on the final wordmark
- **Audio-coupled:** each word slam is a transient hit
- **Transition out:** hold on wordmark to the last frame
